Every session, a language model reads that day's headlines from a single Indonesian financial news feed and files one number: how the market's mood reads, from −100 (panic) to +100 (euphoria), with 0 as neutral. It is a first-pass impression of the news, not a measurement of the market itself — the same headlines read twice, or read by a different model, would not always file the same number.

Note

Terms of the Reading

One feed · one model · one call a day

Source. CNBC Indonesia's general market feed — a mix of genuine market news and unrelated stories (disasters, consumer banking tips). The model is asked to weigh only what is actually market-relevant and ignore the rest.

Judge. One call to a Claude model per day, not an ensemble and not a re-read for consistency. Treat the number as one reader's first impression, not a poll.

Window. Whatever the feed published for that trading day, in Jakarta time — typically a few dozen headlines at most, and sometimes far fewer.
